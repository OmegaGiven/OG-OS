OG Files is a file manager with the viewers and editors built in. Browse your folders on the left and open what's in them in tabs on the right: PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, photos, video, music, archives and 3D models, all in one window, without hopping between apps.

SEE EVERYTHING
• Tree, list and grid views with photo thumbnails, plus quick filters by kind
• An inspector beside every file: type, size, dates, permissions and what you can do with it
• A details strip that keeps a history of what you opened and changed

DOCUMENTS AND PDFS
• A fast PDF viewer with page thumbnails, zoom and a page editor: reorder, rotate, delete, split and merge
• Built-in PDF tools: compress, repair, OCR text from scans, sign and verify signatures, PDF/A
• Open and edit Word (.docx) documents, read ODT and EPUB
• Convert almost anything to PDF: documents, spreadsheets, Markdown, web pages, e-mails, images

SPREADSHEETS AND TEXT
• View and edit xlsx, ods and csv: cells, rows, columns and sorting
• Markdown with a live preview, and a text editor with syntax colours

PHOTOS, VIDEO AND MUSIC
• Pan and zoom photos, turn a batch of pictures into a PDF, split scanned photos
• Play mp4, m4v and mov, step frame by frame, snapshot a frame, or trim a clip into a new file
• Play music with a waveform you can seek in; edit tags

ARCHIVES, 3D AND DISK SPACE
• Browse zip, tar, 7z and rar archives like folders; extract all or just what you pick
• Orbit around OBJ, STL and glTF models: shaded, wireframe, normals and dimensions
• See what fills your disk with an interactive treemap

SAFE BY DESIGN
Edits never write over your originals unless you choose Save: trims, conversions and exports are new files. Deleting moves things to the Trash, and most actions can be undone.

PRIVATE
No account, no ads, no tracking. OG Files collects no data. Your files stay on your Mac, and the app only opens the folders you grant it.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
OG Files is free and open source (MPL-2.0) and also runs on Windows and Linux.
