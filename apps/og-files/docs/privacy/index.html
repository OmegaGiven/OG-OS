OG Files

Privacy policy

OG Files (the apps for macOS, Windows and Linux) · Updated October 20, 2026

The short version

OG Files collects no data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no trackers, and none of your files are sent to us. The OG Files project runs no servers.

Your files stay on your device

OG Files opens, previews and edits files where they already are. Its own settings, bookmarks, recent places, history and thumbnails are kept on your device: on macOS in the app's container (the Mac App Store version is sandboxed and can only open folders you choose to grant it), on Windows in your AppData folder, on Linux in your home folder. Uninstalling the app and deleting those folders removes them.

When the network is used

OG Files only goes online when you ask it to:

  • Convert to PDF of a web page, or of an HTML or Markdown file that links to remote images, downloads that page or those images from the sites they come from.
  • Network locations (direct-download versions only) connect to the servers you add yourself, such as SFTP, SMB, WebDAV or cloud storage. Passwords you choose to remember are kept in your system's keychain.

Those sites and servers are governed by their own privacy terms.

Children

OG Files collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/OmegaGiven/og-files/issues.
